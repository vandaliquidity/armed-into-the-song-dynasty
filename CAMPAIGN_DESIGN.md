# The Qinghe Chronicle — playable campaign design

Implemented 2026-11-18, v0.22. Three opening missions continue into 27 new chapters. Campaign quantities, schematic geography and the 1120 start are gameplay accommodations. The source is the unfinished serial by 茶根儿; the documented reading ledger reaches IV:165. The last act below connects to the game's existing historical crisis and does not claim a novel ending.

## v0.59 story expansion — 24 November 2026

The calendar now carries ten policy scenes and five character interludes, using the same source boundary documented above. The decisions concern housing and markets, doctrine, the capital office, courier redundancy, Deshun's households, equipment standardisation, mineral procurement, succession in command, northern stockpiles and the post-siege legacy. Each policy has an implemented consequence in existing supply, price, training, research, capacity, visibility, morale or prosperity systems. Short callbacks recall previous choices. Explicit choices and actual battles remain necessary; time does not silently select a policy or win a mission. Previous completed chapters receive no retroactive rewards.

Longcheng now adapts the imprisoned-chief arc with capture, contact and physical extraction. The chief's death is a persistent alternate gameplay outcome, not a resettable target; it changes subsequent assaults into securing the fort and remaining testimony. The new relay encounter adapts the frontier-dispatch arc: escort a vulnerable courier between captured posts, then secure the far post for ten seconds. Attackers are reserved from an actual adjacent Xia garrison; surviving attackers return once. Prior conquest averts the ambush. Both encounters preserve strategic casualties and officer consequences.

Story choices are journalled once, including the existing memorial/rescue, truce, southern audit, occupation and medical branches. The final legacy responds to the existing map siege of Kaifeng. The village tablet is commemorative scenery rather than a free building or productive enterprise. The expansion contains nine authored continuing Chronicle encounters in total. Original dialogue and the relay's tactical form are adaptations, not quotations or claims of verified historical events.

## v0.52 consequence, economy and frontier update — 21 November 2026

The five companions are detached from command and all village passives after defeat. Stretcher parties follow friendly roads to Qinghe one link per month; convalescence begins only on admission and lasts ten months after defeat, five after a victory ending with the companion down. Each loses one grade, never below zero, and must earn the lost grade again. A road cut or occupied home delays care. Recruited officers have permanent death when their actual marked troop figure is killed or their defeated formation loses at least 35% of its men. Remaining ordinary officers can lead manual battles using an existing troop figure and a modest rally skill. Companies preserve names, battle records and merge lineages; eliminated companies and dead officers enter memorial records.

Chapter XI now includes a two-depot relief defence: preserve both granaries for 110 seconds and establish a continuous twelve-second beacon hold. A destroyed depot burns ten percent of the actual home grain reserve once. Chapter XXV reserves 150 grain at Youzhou before a fighting withdrawal; a vulnerable ox cart must cross the bridge and reach extraction. Victory dispatches the same cargo to Linhe. Blocked roads retain the manifest for later dispatch without a second payment; defeat loses the reserved grain. The hostile armies are withdrawn from occupied Xia garrisons. Existing active saves with the corridor consignment already dispatched retain their progress.

Jin has six persistent schematic forward camps, finite starting armies, workshops, paid recruitment and monthly provisions. Historical columns assemble from real camp reserves, with other-camp reinforcements travelling before departure. Independent Jin and Xia columns contest a connected border. A paid frontier trade agreement permits finite-stock purchases, carried along actual roads; hostility blocks delivery. Original Blender meshes and an eight-direction sprite atlas distinguish Jin from Xia. The map now has 128 connected sites, including a Xia frontier outpost; it is not an exact historical border reconstruction.

Restaurant spending is limited by local households and real traffic. Regional merchant contracts reserve finite buyer funds and goods, then pay upon arrival. Song market arsenals manufacture from actual materials; opening the UI cannot restock them. Xia/Qiang raids and invasions spend actual local manpower and equipment; difficulty changes decisions rather than adding invisible troops. Prosperity stages require 45/65/80 prosperity, 1/3/5 supplied enterprises and 100 local grain. Town fronts, an academy, lanterns and crowds appear on existing plots and decline with shortages.

## The campaign loop

Read a short council scene, start the current chapter when ready, work toward its visible objectives in the actual campaign, then accept its consequences. Construction, barracks training, manufacturing and transport run through the existing systems. Starting a chapter never grants its reward. Completion pays explicit local costs once. A chapter with a waiting period starts that clock only when the player begins it.

The persistent world keeps running: monthly orders, seasonal industry, wounded care, NPC production and the dated Jin invasions do not wait for the story. Free frontier operations remain available. Earlier conquests can avert a threatened siege, rather than cause replacement armies to materialise.

## Battles with different jobs

- The mountain stronghold uses the fortress battlefield and an authored, finite bandit force. Actual campaign soldiers fight and take persistent casualties. Failed attempts face the surviving enemy pool.
- The royal-road rescue starts with three vulnerable people. Reaching them begins their physical evacuation; at least one must reach extraction. Killing every attacker alone does not win it.
- The river crossing requires both bridgeheads and a continuous 25-second hold. Xia participants are deducted in full from an adjacent Xia garrison. If the neighbouring threat is already gone, consolidation replaces an invented battle.
- Western siege, supply-node, Roulang, Linhe and Youzhou chapters use real campaign ownership, roads and the regular attack/occupation systems. These are not detached combat arenas that reset their outcomes.

## Economy and command

The mission board links directly to the shared production council, town businesses, muster roll and wounded evacuation. Equipment already purchased or gifted may meet reserve requirements, but only actual workshop output advances manufacturing counters. Only real training completion advances recruitment counters. Trade income counts actual export payments.

Chapter appointments add command capacity and identifiable officers. Longcheng, Deshun, Linhe and Youzhou charters conserve their existing Song garrisons. The previously requested once-only 200-militia settlement grant remains an explicit difficulty accommodation. Existing opening troop commissions are preserved as opening support; this design does not present their quantities as novel facts.

Story cargo is loaded from a player-controlled town holding every item and its fee. It follows friendly roads, pauses or reroutes around danger and credits its objective only after arrival. A surviving cart can be redirected to a refuge after Kaifeng falls; it does not duplicate its cargo. A captured destination cannot remain a safe delivery objective. Unfulfilled capital institutions can be established at a surviving friendly administration instead.

## Adaptation boundaries

The source order, institutional progression, industry, major campaigns, political dependence and personal losses guide the campaign. Dialogue is newly written for the game. Existing shortened/altered lead names remain, and added narrative commanders use short aliases.

The source's civilian killings, coerced actions and political impunity are not converted into routine rewards for extermination. The southern inquiry offers an explicitly alternate public audit. Lan's survival and delegated hospital leadership are marked alternate branches. Choices are stored in the journal, rather than silently presented as source canon.

Roulang, Linhe and Deshun are connected schematic campaign locations. The royal-road encounter launches from Qinzhou as an expedition staging point; it does not claim the source's royal rescue happened in Qinzhou. Hangzhou's off-map commercial investigation is represented through the capital dispatch network, not an invented exact route across the current western map.

The adaptation currently uses documented events through IV:165. Later reading and future serial chapters can extend the historical approach; the game does not fabricate their canonical resolution.

## Save and verification contract

The three opening completion flags remain authoritative. Existing campaigns unlock Chapter IV without replaying an optional escort. No migration resets inventories, troops, wounded, towns, commissions or the campaign date. Active chapter, chosen branch, deliveries, enemy survivors and completed rewards are save-backed. Repeated result, arrival and completion callbacks cannot duplicate soldiers, equipment or costs.

Browser checks cover the whole objective schema, chapter ordering, appointment conservation, finite retry forces, actual rescue/extraction, paid Xia deployment, blocked/delivered cargo, production/training counters, late-crisis alternatives and save/load. Existing campaign, economy, medical, battle, mission and banner regressions remain part of the suite.
