# Song visual assets, v0.61

Created 2026-11-25 with the built-in image generation tool, using the imagegen skill. No API CLI or external asset pack was used. Original generated output is retained in the Codex generated-images directory; the selected RGBA PNGs are bundled in this repository:

| Asset | Contents | Integration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `assets/song/architecture-v2.png` | Eight architectural models, 4 columns by 2 rows | Map settlement compositions, village homes, civic hall, smithy, treasury, commerce/production cards |
| `assets/song/landscape-v1.png` | Three ink-painted terrain groups | Existing campaign mountain positions, with renderer-side edge feathering |
| `assets/song/workshops-v1.png` | Six workshops, 3 columns by 2 rows | Village industries and matching construction/commerce cards |

`song-art.js` splits atlas cells at load, trims transparent margins, caches scale variants and retains the established world anchors. The source PNGs are not retouched by scripts. Existing workers, cargo, quantities, interaction boxes and movement remain owned by their existing simulation modules. Props such as tools and hanging sample armour identify a trade; they are not additional playable inventory. The treasury's base image has empty trays; only live balances add bullion. Workshops load on demand. Existing art is used while images are unavailable.

## Visual references

- [Palace Museum: Zhang Zeduan, Qingming scroll](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/paint/228226.html). Northern Song street, shop, transport and architectural reference.
- [Palace Museum digital scroll presentation](https://www.dpm.org.cn/vr/stone-moat/wyd_17.html). Reference for timber buildings and the relationship between countryside, commerce and city streets.

This is an original Song-inspired game interpretation. It is not a traced museum image, a reconstruction of a particular surviving building, or a representation that every listed workshop model was common at this specific frontier location in 1120. The game's alternate-history ordnance development remains distinct from documentary history.

Northern Song Qingming-scroll inspired vernacular, realistic proportions, readable at small game scale, attractive earthy amber/charcoal/celadon palette. Not flat icons and not toy blocks. ## Landscape generation prompt

Use case: historical-scene. Production sprite atlas for a Song dynasty strategy campaign map. One wide landscape sheet, exactly THREE equal columns and ONE row, each containing a distinct isolated Chinese mountain-and-woodland cluster, all fully within its own cell with transparent gutters. True transparent background. No land rectangle, no page texture, no text, no roads, no settlements, no water outside clusters. Hand-painted Northern Song landscape miniature style: fine dark ink rock texture strokes, weathered ochre rock faces and restrained blue-green mineral colour, scattered small dark pine trees, layered ridgelines and translucent soft mist at the foot of the mountains which fades entirely to transparency. Left cell: tall folded grey-green rocky Liupan-like peaks with pines, asymmetrical and naturally eroded. Middle cell: lower warm ochre loess ridges and sparse scrub, arid northwest frontier. Right cell: broad layered blue-green distant mountain range with several wooded summits. Each group has a broad natural base and multiple irregular summits, not triangular geometric cones. Detail readable at small scale; refined illustrated map terrain rather than cinematic fantasy scenery. Consistent lighting upper-left, oblique aerial view, all groups occupy the bottom two thirds of their cells, with a little clear air above. No square bases, no decorative frames, no characters or symbols, no houses or temples.
